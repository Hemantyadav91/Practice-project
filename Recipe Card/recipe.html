Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked quickly with butter in a hot frying pan, optionally filled with cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total:Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation:5 minutes
  • Cooking5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced ham, onions, tomatoes, spinach

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs:In a bowl, beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until they are gently blended. You can add a tablespoon of water or milk for a fluffier texture.
  2. Heat the pan:Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and melt the butter.
  3. Cook the omelette:Once the bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan gently to spread the eggs evenly.
  4. Add fillings (optional):When the eggs begin to set at the edges but are still slightly runny in the middle, sprinkle you fillings over one half.
  5. Fold and serve:Gently fold the omelette in half. Slide it onto a plate and enjoy hot!
